📖 Understanding the Stats

What each stat means for your fighter.
Dexterity
Represents the fighter's precision, technique, and ability to execute attacks accurately and efficiently.
Agility
Represents the fighter's movement, speed, reflexes, and ability to evade or reposition quickly.
Stamina
Represents how well the fighter can maintain their performance over time and continue fighting without getting tired quickly.
Power
Represents the strength and impact of the fighter's attacks, especially the force behind their punches.
Endurance
Represents how well the fighter can withstand damage, pressure, and powerful attacks without losing effectiveness.
